# AI Marketing Toolkit

An open-source collection of AI prompts, marketing templates, workflows, and guides for freelancers, agencies, students, and small businesses.

## Features

- Meta Ads Prompts
- Google Ads Prompts
- Lead Generation Templates
- Cold Outreach Templates
- Email Marketing Templates
- SEO Prompts
- Content Marketing Resources

## Project Goals

- Help marketers use AI effectively.
- Share free marketing resources.
- Build a community-driven open-source toolkit.
