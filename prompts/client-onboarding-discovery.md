# Client Onboarding & Discovery Prompts

## Prompt 1

Create a comprehensive client discovery questionnaire for a [business type]. Cover business goals, target audience, products/services, competitors, current marketing, budget, challenges, and expectations.

## Prompt 2

Create a client onboarding checklist for a digital marketing agency managing Meta Ads and Google Ads.

## Prompt 3

Generate 20 discovery-call questions that help a marketer understand a client's business, customers, offers, sales process, and marketing goals.

## Prompt 4

Create a marketing brief template that summarizes the client's business, target audience, positioning, offer, competitors, campaign objectives, and KPIs.

## Prompt 5

Create a list of documents and account access a client should provide before starting a digital marketing project.

## Prompt 6

Create a client expectation-setting document for performance marketing. Explain realistic timelines, testing, optimization, reporting, client responsibilities, and factors that can affect results.

## Prompt 7

Create a campaign kickoff meeting agenda for a new performance marketing client.

## Prompt 8

Create a client feedback questionnaire to evaluate communication, reporting, campaign management, creative quality, and overall service.

## Prompt 9

Create a client offboarding checklist for a completed digital marketing project. Include account access, reports, campaign data, assets, documentation, and final communication.
