# Ad Creative & Content Angle Prompts

## Prompt 1

Generate 10 different Meta Ads creative angles for a [business/product]. Include the audience problem, core message, visual concept, hook, and CTA.

## Prompt 2

Create 10 problem-awareness ad concepts for [product/service]. Focus on the customer's pain points without directly selling the product.

## Prompt 3

Create 10 solution-awareness ad concepts showing how [product/service] solves common customer problems.

## Prompt 4

Create 10 product-awareness ad concepts highlighting the features, benefits, use cases, and differentiators of [product/service].

## Prompt 5

Create 10 testimonial-based ad concepts for [business/product]. Include the hook, story structure, proof, and CTA.

## Prompt 6

Generate 10 comparison-based ad concepts that position [product/service] against common alternatives without making unsupported claims.

## Prompt 7

Create 10 educational Reel concepts for [business type]. Include a strong first-three-second hook, video structure, on-screen text, and CTA.

## Prompt 8

Generate 10 UGC-style video ad scripts for [product/service]. Make them natural, conversational, and focused on a specific customer problem.

## Prompt 9

Create 10 static-image ad concepts for [business/product]. For each concept include headline, supporting text, visual direction, and CTA.

## Prompt 10

Generate 5 complete creative testing variations for the same Meta Ads campaign. Keep the offer constant but change the hook, angle, format, and messaging.
