# Local Business Marketing Prompts

## Prompt 1

Create a complete local marketing strategy for a [business type] targeting customers in [city/area].

## Prompt 2

Identify the ideal local customer for a [business type]. Include location, demographics, needs, problems, buying intent, and decision factors.

## Prompt 3

Create 20 Google Business Profile post ideas for a [business type]. Include offers, educational posts, updates, FAQs, and customer-focused content.

## Prompt 4

Create a local SEO strategy for a [business type] targeting [location]. Include keyword ideas, Google Business Profile optimization, reviews, local content, and citations.

## Prompt 5

Generate 10 local Meta Ads creative angles for a [business type]. Focus on location, local problems, offers, trust, and convenience.

## Prompt 6

Create a customer review generation strategy for a local business. Include ethical ways to request reviews and follow-up messages.

## Prompt 7

Write 10 Google Business Profile review response templates for positive, neutral, and negative customer reviews.

## Prompt 8

Create a local lead-generation funnel for a [business type]. Include advertisement, landing page or lead form, follow-up, appointment booking, and conversion.

## Prompt 9

Generate 20 content ideas that make a local [business type] look trustworthy and established in the community.

## Prompt 10

Create a 30-day local marketing plan for a [business type] in [location]. Include Google Business Profile, SEO, social media, paid advertising, reviews, and customer retention.
