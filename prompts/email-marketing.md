Email Marketing Prompts

Prompt 1

Write a 5-email welcome sequence for a new lead who has subscribed to a business newsletter. Include subject lines, engaging copy, value, and clear CTAs.

Prompt 2

Create a promotional email campaign for a service business launching a limited-time offer. Include 5 subject lines, preview text, email body, CTA, and urgency without sounding spammy.

Prompt 3

Write a 5-email nurture sequence to convert cold leads into customers. Use different angles such as problem awareness, education, social proof, objections, and a final offer.

Prompt 4

Create a follow-up email sequence for leads who showed interest in a service but did not book a consultation. Include messages for Day 1, Day 3, Day 7, and Day 14.

Prompt 5

Write a re-engagement email campaign for inactive subscribers. Create 3 emails designed to identify their interests, provide value, and encourage them to stay subscribed.

Prompt 6

Create an abandoned-lead follow-up email sequence for prospects who started a lead form or inquiry but did not complete the process. Include persuasive CTAs and objection handling.
