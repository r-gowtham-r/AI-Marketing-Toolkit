# Marketing Strategy & Planning Prompts

## Prompt 1

Create a complete digital marketing strategy for a [business type] targeting [target audience] in [location].

## Prompt 2

Create a 90-day marketing plan for a [business type]. Divide the plan into awareness, consideration, lead generation, conversion, and retention.

## Prompt 3

Identify the ideal customer profile for a [business type]. Include demographics, interests, pain points, buying motivations, objections, and purchase triggers.

## Prompt 4

Create a customer journey for a [business type] from first discovering the brand to becoming a paying customer.

## Prompt 5

Create a full-funnel marketing strategy for a [business type]. Include TOFU, MOFU, and BOFU campaigns with suitable content and offers.

## Prompt 6

Analyze the following business and identify its strongest marketing opportunities, weaknesses, competitive advantages, and growth opportunities:

[Business details]

## Prompt 7

Create a marketing strategy for a local business targeting customers within [location]. Include channels, content strategy, advertising strategy, offers, and lead-generation methods.

## Prompt 8

Create 5 unique marketing offers for a [business type] that can increase lead generation and conversions without relying only on discounts.

## Prompt 9

Create a competitor analysis framework for a [business type]. Compare competitors based on positioning, offers, pricing, creatives, content, customer reviews, and marketing channels.

## Prompt 10

Create a complete marketing action plan for the next 30 days for a [business type]. Prioritize activities based on potential impact, effort, and expected results.
